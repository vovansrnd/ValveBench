   t = (start_frame + np.arange(n)) / fs.
   For a persistent sinusoid, the fitted absolute-time phase should be constant modulo 2π across windows, not advance by 2π f hop (12.08 rad at a 125 ms hop). The report's comparison to a 12.08 rad expected step is invalid. The observed early-window phase estimates change; however the windows overlap by 75%, and the fitted amplitude rapidly approaches the noise floor. It is not a measured physical sinusoid envelope. Moreover, −72.20 to −116.58 dBFS is a 44.38 dB fall over 0.5 s, i.e. about −88.8 dB/s as a simple average slope, not −45 dB/s.
